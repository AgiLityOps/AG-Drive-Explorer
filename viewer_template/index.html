Controls

Click viewCapture mouse (look around). Esc releases it
W A S D / arrowsFly forward / left / back / right
Space or EUp
C or QDown
Shift (hold)Boost x5
Mouse wheelChange fly speed
Drag (without capture)Look around
RBack to the start line
FToggle "ride the racing line" (follows the game's racing line with its banking; the speed slider sets the ride speed, Shift boosts, the mouse looks around)
VWhile riding: look straight ahead again
NToggle animations (flying ships, moving towers, traffic lanes, cycling ad screens)
GToggle fog
[ and ] or PgUp / PgDnPrevious / next track
1-9, 0, -, =Jump straight to track 1-12
HShow / hide this help

Geometry, textures, skyboxes, fog and lighting values are converted from the AG Drive 1.4.5 game files.
